The license applicable to users of KillMutants is separate from the exploitation license granted to REEFACT.

## Contributions

External contributions are governed by the [KillMutants Contributor Agreement](./CONTRIBUTOR_AGREEMENT.md).

Under that agreement, the transferable economic rights necessary to own, maintain, relicense, and commercially exploit accepted contributions are assigned to the Project Owner, with a broad fallback license where assignment is not legally available.

A contribution made directly by the Project Owner through `@Reefact` does not require the Project Owner to enter into the Contributor Agreement with himself.

Pull requests produced through the Project Owner's Claude Code workflow remain subject to the repository's explicit contributor-agreement acknowledgement check as a project-control rule.
